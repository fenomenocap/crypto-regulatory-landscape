## What this repo is

**Crypto Regulatory Atlas** is a zero-dependency static site: one `index.html` with inlined HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS. It visualizes the global crypto regulatory landscape across 30 jurisdictions in 5 regions — an interactive world map, regional card grid, search/filter, and per-country dossiers (regulators, frameworks, license types, requirements, operator notes). No build step; deploy as static files (Vercel recommended).
